Our Mission & Track Record

Established in 2017, Trannity is a verified non-profit dedicated to serving less privileged children and families across the United States and Canada, in strategic operational partnership with Save the Children and ShelterBox.

Feed The American Kids

$850,000 Raised • 200,000 kids provided with emergency weekend food security kits.

Project Sunflower

$2.5M Raised • 2,000 low-income families provided with winter heating and housing aid.

Project Tulip

$300,000 Raised • 90 nursing homes supplied with healthcare materials and companionship.

Why Your Donation Matters
